01

Tokenise & tag

sent_tokenize, word_tokenize and pos_tag split the text into sentences and words, then tag every word (NNP = proper noun).

02

Recognise entities

ne_chunk plus our own rules find people, places and organisations. On unseen texts the rules lift F1 from 0.33 to 0.93.

03

Build facts

Each verb becomes a fact: who did what, where, when and for how much. Regular expressions catch dates, phones, emails and amounts.
