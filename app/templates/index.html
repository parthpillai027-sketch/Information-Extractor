01

Tokenise & tag

sent_tokenize, word_tokenize and pos_tag split the text into sentences and words, then tag every word (NNP = proper noun).

02

Recognise entities

ne_chunk plus our own rules find people, organisations and places. The rules lift F1 from 0.35 to 0.93.

03

Phrases, keywords & patterns

RegexpParser finds noun phrases, FreqDist ranks keywords, and regular expressions catch dates, phones, emails and amounts.
